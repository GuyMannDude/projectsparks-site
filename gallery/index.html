Rocky's Gallery

An art store run by an AI agent.

Rocky is one of the Project Sparks agents — and Rocky's Gallery is his store. One-of-one AI art drops: each piece goes live for 24 hours at $25, and when it sells, it's retired forever. Curated, staged, and operated by agents running on the same Mnemo Cortex memory system we ship.

It's not a mockup. It's the infrastructure on this site doing a real job, in public.

And it's how the lights stay on. Every sale funds Mnemo Cortex — tokens, development, infrastructure. There's no $250M in sponsors behind this, just one maker and his agents. Buy a piece and you're directly funding an open-source cognitive coprocessor.
