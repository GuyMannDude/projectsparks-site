Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. The short version

We don't sell anything, so we collect no payment details and use no payment processor. We run no analytics and set no tracking cookies. We have no account system, no mailing list, and no advertising. The only things we handle are the emails you choose to send us and the messages you type into the chat bubble.

2. Visiting the site

This site is served by Google Firebase Hosting, which keeps standard server logs including IP addresses, as any web host does. We don't add analytics on top of that, and we don't build profiles of visitors.

Most pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the home page shows a star-count badge from shields.io. Loading those means your browser contacts Google and shields.io directly, so those services see your IP address under their own privacy policies. Nothing about you is sent to them by us.

3. The chat assistant — read this bit

If you use the chat bubble, this is what happens to what you type:

It goes to an AI provider. Your message is sent to our server, which forwards it to OpenRouter, which routes it to the language model that writes the reply. Those providers process your message under their own policies. Don't type anything into the chat you wouldn't want a third party to process — no passwords, no personal details, nothing confidential.

The conversation is logged. We keep a plain-text log of chat messages and replies, with a timestamp, on our own machine. There is no identifier attached to it — we can read what was asked, not who asked it. We use it to find questions the assistant answered badly and fix them.

It does not remember you. Nothing about you is stored between conversations, deliberately. Every chat starts fresh. There is no visitor profile, because we don't keep one.

In your browser: the open conversation is held in your tab's session storage so it survives page navigation, and it is gone when you close the tab. We set no cookies.

4. Donations

Tip links point to PayPal, Ko-fi and GitHub Sponsors. Payments happen entirely on their sites under their privacy policies. We never see or store your card details, and we don't receive a customer list.

5. Email

If you email us, we keep that email so we can reply and so we remember the conversation later. We won't add you to a list or pass it to anyone.

Day-to-day operations here are run by a human working alongside AI assistants, and an AI may help draft a reply to you. A human is accountable for anything sent under our name.

6. What we never do

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't run ad trackers. We don't send marketing you didn't ask for. We're a one-person operation that gives away software and art — there is no data business here to feed.

7. Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it, and we'll do it by hand because that's the size we are. For the chat log, tell us roughly when you chatted and what about, and we'll find and remove it — since we store no identifier, that's the only way we can locate it.
